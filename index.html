Generator fuel during blackouts

During each blackout hour inside opening hours the point runs on a petrol generator. Fuel per hour depends on how loaded the generator is: the effective load (each item’s kW × its average use) divided by the generator’s rated output, interpolated between its consumption at 50% and at 100% load. All inputs are assumptions; edit them per point in each point’s inputs below.

How the fuel is calculated

Load ratio = effective load ÷ rated kW. Fuel l/h = l/h at 50% + (l/h at 100% − l/h at 50%) × (ratio − 0.5) ÷ 0.5, extended below 50% along the same line (never below 0) and capped at the 100% figure. Litres = blackout hours × l/h. Cost = litres × price + hours × oil & maintenance. kWh = effective load × hours. Revenue is assumed unchanged during blackouts (the point keeps trading on the generator); grid utilities are not reduced.

Tax (ФОП), shared by all points

Group 3: single tax and military levy as a % of each point’s revenue. Groups 1 and 2: fixed ₴/month amounts instead. The owner’s ЄСВ applies under every group. As in the original pages, each point’s model carries its own ЄСВ and fixed tax; see the note below.

Tax rates

Group limits (2026). Group 1: income up to ₴1 444 049 a year, no hired employees, individuals only as buyers, no РРО/ПРРО needed. Group 2: up to ₴7 211 598 a year (834 minimum wages), up to 10 employees, buyers individuals and single-tax payers, restaurant/café services allowed. Group 1 fixed tax up to ₴332.80/month (10% of the ₴3 328 subsistence minimum), group 2 up to ₴1 729.40/month (20% of the ₴8 647 minimum wage); the local council sets the exact rate. Military levy ₴864.70/month for groups 1 and 2, 1% of revenue for group 3. ЄСВ minimum ₴1 902.34/month. Warnings compare annualised Apr – Sep revenue (× 2) with the limit, for each point and for the three points combined, since the limit applies to the owner’s total income.

ЄСВ and fixed tax are counted once per point. Each point’s model includes the owner’s ЄСВ (and, under groups 1 – 2, the fixed single tax and levy), exactly as in the original single-point pages. For one ФОП owning all three points these are paid once, so the combined total understates cash by about over Apr – Sep. Not adjusted here; see open questions.

Method & sources

Cash. Monthly cash = revenue − barista − variable costs − fixed costs − generator. Before depreciation, loans and the owner’s own time. Cash on hand is the running sum of monthly cash from ₴0 on 1 Apr 2026. All figures in UAH.

Average check what-if. Moves every month’s average check by the same %; receipts stay at their recorded counts, so revenue moves by the same %. Cost of goods and other revenue-linked costs scale with it (a bigger check means more items per order). A pure price rise would lower the cost-of-goods %; test that with each point’s override. Break-even avg check = the check at which that point’s Apr – Sep cash is zero.

Blackout hours. No official Укренерго forecast for winter 2026 – 27 was found. Experts quoted in October 2026 expect morning and evening outages of 2 – 4 hours at −10 °C without new strikes (УНІАН), and up to 6 hours a day in some cities (Главред). The default of 60 h a month (≈2 h each open day) is a round assumption, applied to every month.

Equipment defaults. 2-group espresso machine 4.5 kW (3 – 5 kW typical for 2 groups, ADS Coffee Supplies), heaters on ≈30% of the time (20 – 35% duty for commercial machines, Pro Coffee Gear). Grinder 0.35 kW each (Mazzer Super Jolly), running ≈10% of the time. Under-counter fridge 0.2 kW at 30% (ENERGY STAR units average 35 – 40 W; door openings add more). LED lighting, POS and router at 100%. Generator: 6 kW rated, 2.5 l/h at full load (MB7000E-A spec), 1.6 l/h at 50% (60 – 70% of full-load use, Rental Power).
